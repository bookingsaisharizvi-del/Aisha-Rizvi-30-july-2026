An engagement look is its own brief

New York engagement parties tend to be shorter and more tightly lit than a wedding — a restaurant, a hall, a rooftop — and the photography is mostly phones rather than a hired photographer. That shifts the priorities. The look needs to read well at arm's length and in artificial light, and it needs to survive a room that gets warm quickly once it fills up.

What an engagement booking includes

  • Full application — prep, long-wear base, eyes, lashes and lip, finished for indoor and evening light
  • A look you choose — from understated to full glam, since this event sets no rules
  • Outfit coordination — planned around your engagement outfit and jewellery rather than a default palette
  • Mother and sister makeup — added to the same appointment where needed
  • Touch-up kit — to carry through the evening

Engagement makeup questions

Do you travel from New Jersey into New York for engagement parties?

Yes. Aisha is based in Iselin, NJ and takes bookings across New York including Manhattan, Queens, Brooklyn and Long Island. Arrival time is agreed in advance so travel is accounted for.

Is engagement makeup different from bridal makeup?

Usually lighter and less formal, and there is more freedom in the look. Many brides treat it as a chance to test how a style photographs before committing to it for the wedding.

Can you also do makeup for the dholki or Mangni?

Yes. These smaller pre-wedding events can be booked individually or alongside the engagement, and the looks are planned so they do not repeat each other.
