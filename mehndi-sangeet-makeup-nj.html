Mehndi & Sangeet · New Jersey

Mehndi & Sangeet Makeup Artist in New Jersey

Mehndi night is the one event where nobody is sitting still. There is dancing, there is heat from a packed room, there is a camera out continuously for five or six hours, and your hands are occupied from the moment the henna starts. The makeup has to be built for that — brighter and more playful than the ceremony look, and set so you are not fixing it at midnight. Aisha Rizvi Artistry creates Mehndi and Sangeet makeup on location across New Jersey.

Makeup built for a long, warm, moving night

Colour on a Mehndi night usually comes from the outfit — yellows, greens, oranges, mirror work — so the makeup is balanced against it rather than competing with it. Practically, two things decide whether the look survives: how it is set, and how little you have to touch it. With henna on your hands, touching up is not really an option, so the look is finished to hold on its own.

What a Mehndi or Sangeet booking covers

  • Transfer-resistant base — set so it holds through heat, dancing and a crowded room
  • Festive eyes — brighter, bolder work than the ceremony look, with lashes secured for the night
  • Colour matched to the outfit — the look is planned around what you are actually wearing, not a stock palette
  • Hands-free wear — finished so it needs no touch-ups once your henna is on
  • Family and bridal party — coordinated looks for mothers, sisters and friends, scheduled in sequence

Why this event needs its own plan

Longest event of the week

A Mehndi routinely runs past midnight. The products are chosen for that length, not for a two-hour party.

Henna changes everything

Once henna is on, your hands are out of action. The look is set to need nothing from you afterwards.

Video as much as photos

Mehndi nights get filmed continuously, so the finish is checked for movement and flash, not just a still frame.

Planned with the other events

The Mehndi look is designed to contrast with your Nikaah and Baraat looks rather than repeat them.

Booking Mehndi or Sangeet makeup

Aisha works by appointment from 1315 Oak Tree Rd, Iselin, NJ, and travels throughout New Jersey, New York and Pennsylvania. Please note that Aisha provides makeup artistry; henna application itself is arranged separately through your henna artist.

  1. Consultation. Talk through the outfit, the venue and how many people need makeup that evening.

  4. Carry it into the week. If you are booking several events, each look is planned as part of one progression.

Mehndi and Sangeet makeup questions

Do you apply henna as well as makeup?

No. Aisha Rizvi Artistry provides makeup artistry only. Henna application is arranged separately with a henna artist, and the makeup timing is planned around when your henna is being done.

Will the makeup last through a full night of dancing?

It is set specifically for that. Long-wear products are used throughout and the look is finished so it does not need touching up once henna is on your hands.

Can the bridal party be booked for the same evening?

Yes. Makeup for mothers, sisters and friends can be added and scheduled in sequence around the bride so the whole group is ready on time.
